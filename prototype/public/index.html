How the shop works

Prep

No clock is running. Order stock at the terminal in storage or press O anywhere. Carry boxes to fixtures and press E. When you are ready press Enter to open the doors.

The till

Stand on the mat behind a till. Tap Space to scan each item, then once more to ask for payment. Card and UPI approve on their own. For cash, the drawer opens: count out the change with notes and coins and press Enter. Short-changing annoys shoppers; giving too much costs you.

Trouble

A red THIEF tag means run them down and press E - the goods drop as boxes. Spills get a yellow sign: walk up and press E three times. Both cost reputation if ignored.

Shelves

The first product stocked claims a fixture. Look at a stocked fixture empty-handed and press R to take everything back into a box so you can reuse it.

Growing

You start as a small corner shop. Serve shoppers and build reputation, then expand (U): Mini Market, Supermarket, Superstore. Each level opens new aisles and tills. Buy product licences to sell more lines, and hire staff to work on their own.

Weather

Rain means fewer shoppers and more spills. Festivals bring crowds. Heatwaves empty the fridges and freezers.
